# How to upload the clip (for Jhon — from your phone)

Deadline: **before 23 Nov 2026 08:00 WIB**. Upload as early as possible —
places go by YouTube views, so every hour counts. Do NOT buy views or use
bots — bought/botted views disqualify the entry.

## 1. Get the clip file onto your phone

The clip is `clip/gta6-vice-city-short.mp4` in this repo (30 seconds,
1080×1920 vertical, ready for YouTube Shorts). Download it from the GitHub
repo page on your phone, or take the file the agent sends you.

## 2. Upload to YouTube

1. Open the **YouTube app** → tap **+** → **Upload a video** → pick
   `gta6-vice-city-short.mp4`.
2. Title (copy exactly):

   `GTA 6 is HERE — Welcome Back to Vice City!`
3. Description — paste EXACTLY this (both lines, nothing changed):

   ```
   Made by my AI agent for a $500 bounty on make.fun, where you can earn money making clips with AI: https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-clipper-01
   makefun-sail-mitten-reindeer
   ```

   Line 1 is the required bounty line (word for word). Line 2 is the play
   code — it must appear exactly as written.
4. Visibility: **Public** (NOT unlisted, NOT private).
5. Audience: "No, it's not made for kids". Add tags if you like: GTA6,
   GTA 6, Vice City, Shorts.
6. Publish. A clip under 60 seconds in vertical format becomes a Short.

## 3. Send the video link back to the agent

After publishing, tap **Share → Copy link** on the video and send the link
to the agent. The agent will then:

- put the real link into `VIDEO.json` (replacing the
  `REPLACE_WITH_VIDEO_ID` placeholder),
- re-run the checks, and
- submit the entry (repo + video link + transcripts) before the deadline.

## 4. Keep it public

Leave the video **Public** until the results are out. A video gone or no
longer public at the deadline (23 Nov 08:00 WIB) takes no place.
